Business Line of Credit FAQs

Know the structure before you commit.

Clear answers to the questions that matter before choosing this capital path.

How does a business line of credit work?+

An approved line provides access up to a limit. The business draws as needed and repays according to the provider’s agreement; reusable availability may replenish after repayment.

Do I pay on the full limit?+

Many lines calculate interest or fees based on amounts drawn, but unused-line, maintenance or other fees may still apply depending on the provider.

Can the line be renewed?+

Some products renew or remain open subject to account performance and periodic review. Renewal is not automatic unless the agreement says so.

How is a limit determined?+

Providers may consider revenue, deposits, operating history, credit, industry, existing debt and requested use.

Is a line better than a term loan?+

A line is generally better suited to recurring or uncertain needs. A term loan may fit a defined, one-time investment more cleanly.
