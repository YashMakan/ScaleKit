Know what Scalekit does, what lenders decide and what to expect before you apply.

Is Scalekit a lender?+

No. Scalekit is a business-financing platform. We connect qualified businesses with financing options from our network of 200+ lenders and help you understand the available paths.

Will checking my options affect my credit?+

The initial eligibility review is designed to identify possible options. Some lender processes may include a hard credit inquiry before final approval. You will be informed before that occurs.

How quickly can I receive funding?+

Funding takes approximately 14 days on average. The actual timeline depends on the financing product, lender, documentation and applicant qualifications.

How much funding can I qualify for?+

Available amounts vary based on business revenue, time in business, credit profile, cash flow, industry and the specific financing product.

What documents should I prepare?+

Most applicants should be ready with recent business bank statements, identification, entity documents and basic revenue information. Additional documents may be required by the lender.
