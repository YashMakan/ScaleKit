Working capital FAQs

Clarity before commitment.

The essential questions to answer before choosing an operating-capital product.

What is working-capital financing?+

Working-capital financing is capital used to support day-to-day operations and near-term business needs, including payroll, rent, vendors, inventory and short-term growth opportunities.

What type of working capital is best?+

It depends on whether the need is recurring or one-time, how quickly the capital produces revenue, and what repayment structure your cash flow can support.

How long does funding take?+

Funding takes approximately 14 days on average across Scalekit clients. The actual timeline varies by lender, product, documentation and applicant profile.

Does Scalekit decide whether I am approved?+

No. Approval, rates, amounts and final terms are determined by the third-party financing provider. Scalekit helps you identify and navigate potential options.

Can I check multiple financing paths?+

Yes. Scalekit's role is to help qualified businesses evaluate relevant paths across a network of 200+ lenders rather than forcing every business into one product.
