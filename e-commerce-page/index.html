E-commerce funding FAQs

Know the structure before you use the capital.

Clear answers for operators evaluating their next funding move.

What can e-commerce financing be used for?+

Common uses include inventory purchases, supplier deposits, advertising, shipping, marketplace expansion and seasonal preparation. The permitted use ultimately depends on the financing product and lender.

What does Scalekit evaluate?+

Potential options may consider revenue, business age, deposit consistency, credit profile, platform performance, industry and requested amount.

How quickly can an e-commerce business get funded?+

Scalekit clients are funded in approximately 14 days on average. Complete documentation and a strong profile can help keep the process moving.

Will I give up equity?+

Debt and credit products generally do not require ownership equity. However, repayment terms, guarantees and security requirements vary by lender and product.

Is Scalekit the lender?+

No. Scalekit helps qualified businesses identify and navigate options from third-party financing providers in our lender network.
